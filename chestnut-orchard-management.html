Common questions

What growers want to know.

Have another question? Talk to us.

Can RootedField track chestnut cultivars?

Yes. Individual tree records can include variety or cultivar information.

Is RootedField only for mature orchards?

No. The same tree records can be used during orchard establishment and continue as trees mature and begin producing.

Does RootedField tell me exactly how much to water?

No. RootedField can help organize watering history and weather-driven water-need context, but it does not pretend to know an exact irrigation volume when the necessary site-specific inputs are unavailable.

Can I map individual chestnut trees?

Yes. RootedField supports GPS-based orchard mapping and individual tree locations.
