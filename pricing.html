Good to know

Start free. Change as you grow.

Need help choosing? Talk to us.

Can I try a paid plan before subscribing?

Yes. Pro and Team Pro include a 14-day free trial, and no credit card is required to start.

What happens when I reach my tree limit?

You can move to the next plan whenever your orchard needs more capacity. Your existing records stay together in RootedField.

Can I add more people to my orchard?

Yes. Pro includes one seat and Team Pro includes five. Additional users are $5 per month.

Which plans include mapping and activity history?

GPS mapping, full activity history, and reports are included with Pro and Team Pro.
