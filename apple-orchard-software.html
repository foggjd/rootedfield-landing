Common questions

What growers want to know.

Have another question? Talk to us.

Can I track apple varieties separately?

Yes. RootedField tree records support variety information, making it possible to organize and filter trees by the varieties you grow.

Does RootedField diagnose apple diseases?

RootedField can provide weather-based disease context for supported models, but it does not diagnose infection or replace scouting and professional agronomic advice.

Can I record harvests by tree?

Yes. Harvest activities can be associated with individual tree records, helping build production history over time.

Can I use RootedField in the orchard on my phone?

Yes. RootedField is web-based and designed to work across phones, tablets, and desktop computers.
