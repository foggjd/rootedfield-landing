Have another question? Talk to us.

What is orchard management software?

Orchard management software keeps tree records, field activities, schedules, harvest information, maps, and other operational data in a digital system built around orchard work.

Can RootedField replace an orchard spreadsheet?

RootedField is designed to replace many common tree lists, watering logs, activity sheets, and orchard notes with connected records that are easier to search and maintain.

Does RootedField work on a phone?

Yes. RootedField is web-based and works on phones, tablets, and desktop computers.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. Paid plans include a 14-day free trial with no credit card required, and a Free plan is available.
